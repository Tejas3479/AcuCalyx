🔴 Afferent & Efferent Arterioles
High-pressure microvascular feed supplying the glomerular capillary bed and receiving filtered blood exiting into peritubular capillaries.
🟡 Glomerulus & Bowman's Capsule
Spherical filtration tuft enclosed by double-walled epithelial capsule. Filters ~125 mL/min of primary urine driven by hydrostatic pressure.
🟠 Proximal Convoluted Tubule (PCT)
Extensive convoluted cortical segments with brush-border microvilli reabsorbing ~65% of filtered water, sodium, glucose, and amino acids.
🔵 Loop of Henle (Medullary Hairpin)
Deep hairpin loop descending into medullary pyramid. Creates hypertonic interstitial gradient via countercurrent multiplier mechanism.
🟤 Distal Convoluted Tubule (DCT)
Cortical segment mediating hormonal regulation of calcium (PTH) and sodium reabsorption / potassium secretion (Aldosterone).
🏺 Collecting Duct & Papillary Duct
Straight vertical conduit channeling concentrated urine under ADH/vasopressin control down to the cribriform area of the renal papilla.
⚕️ PCNL Access Invariant (Nephron Sparing)
AcuCalyx aligns punctures strictly through the avascular papilla fornix (Brödel's line) to prevent shearing medullary Henle loops and avoid transecting interlobar vascular arcades.
